Discographie / Press Play

PRESS PLAY is LISA's bold follow-up to her debut full-length album - a celebration of how far she's come, and a reminder of where it all began. It's LISA at her most personal, honoring her roots in Thailand and her rise as a global icon.The 6-track EP arrives on the heels of a breakout year that took her from The White Lotus S3 to sold-out Vegas shows to her own documentary premiere at the Toronto International Film Festival.
